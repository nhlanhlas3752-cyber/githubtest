How Results Are Published

When Are Results Available?

Term results are uploaded within 5 school days after the end of each term. You will be notified by your class teacher when they are ready.

Symbol Guide

80–100%: Distinction (D) • 70–79%: Merit (M) • 50–69%: Achieved (A) • Below 50%: Not Yet Achieved (NYA)

Result Not Showing?

If your results do not appear, ensure your name matches your school record exactly. Contact your grade teacher or the school office for assistance.
