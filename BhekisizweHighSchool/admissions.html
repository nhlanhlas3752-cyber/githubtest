How we use this information

The personal information you provide is collected by Bhekisizwe Senior Secondary School solely to assess this admission application and, if successful, to enrol the learner.

It is accessible only to authorised admissions staff, is stored securely, and is kept for as long as required by school policy and applicable law, after which it is deleted.

We do not sell your information or share it with third parties except where required by law. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of this information at any time by contacting admissions@bhekisizwehigh.edu.za.
