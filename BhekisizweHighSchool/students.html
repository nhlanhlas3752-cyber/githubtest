The SRC is the official voice of the student body at Bhekisizwe. Elected annually by their peers, SRC members serve as the bridge between learners and school management, driving initiatives that improve school life for everyone.

Sport at Bhekisizwe builds discipline, teamwork, and resilience. We compete in district and provincial tournaments across multiple codes.

Learner Wellbeing

Support & Wellness

We believe a healthy, supported learner performs at their best. Our wellness programmes ensure no student faces challenges alone.

Academic Support

Peer tutoring, extra classes, and subject-specific workshops for learners who need additional help. Available to all grades free of charge.

Discipline & Values

Code of Conduct

Our code of conduct exists to maintain a safe, respectful, and productive learning environment for every learner and staff member.

Uniform Policy
  • Full school uniform is compulsory every school day.
  • Grey trousers or skirt, white shirt, and school tie for formal days.
  • Sports kit is only permitted during PE and practice sessions.
  • Neat, clean appearance is expected at all times on school property.
  • Jewellery must be minimal — one pair of plain stud earrings allowed.
Attendance & Punctuality
  • Learners must be on school premises by 07:30 each day.
  • Absence must be explained by a written note or parent call within 24 hours.
  • Minimum 80% attendance is required to qualify for year-end examinations.
  • All periods, including afternoon activities, must be attended punctually.
Behaviour & Respect
  • Respectful conduct towards all staff, learners, and school property.
  • Bullying, discrimination, and harassment in any form is strictly prohibited.
  • Mobile phones must be stored away during instructional time.
  • Violence, alcohol, and substances on school property result in suspension.
Academic Integrity
  • All submitted work must be the learner's own original effort.
  • Plagiarism and copying in assessments results in a zero and disciplinary action.
  • Classwork, homework, and projects must be completed on time.
  • Any form of cheating during examinations is a serious offence.
