Frequently Asked

The school office is open Monday to Friday, 07:00 to 16:00. The office is closed on weekends, public holidays, and during school holidays.

Absences must be reported by 08:00 on the day of absence by calling our main office number. A written note or medical certificate must be submitted upon the learner's return.

Report copies can be requested from the Academic Office. Please allow 3–5 working days. A small administrative fee may apply.

Please contact the School Counsellor (Ms. T. Mokoena) directly for sensitive matters. All reports are handled confidentially.

Teaching vacancies are advertised on the DBE website. Please do not contact the school directly about vacancies unless responding to an advertised post.
