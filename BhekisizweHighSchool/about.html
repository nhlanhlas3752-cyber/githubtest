Our History

Founded on the Principles of Excellence

Bhekisizwe Senior Secondary School was established in 2004 by the visionary educator Mr. M.E Mnguni, who believed passionately that every child in the Nqutu community deserved access to world-class education. From its humble beginnings with fewer than 200 learners and a small teaching staff, the school has grown into one of KwaZulu-Natal's most respected academic institutions.

As a 100% Black-owned institution, Bhekisizwe is a testament to the power of community, resilience, and an unrelenting pursuit of excellence. Over the decades, we have produced thousands of graduates who have gone on to lead careers in medicine, law, engineering, education, business, and public service — each carrying forward the values instilled within these walls.

Mr. M.E Mnguni

Our Journey

Milestones Through the Years

2004

School Founded

Mr. M.E Mnguni establishes Bhekisizwe Senior Secondary School with 120 learners and a founding staff of 8 dedicated educators.

2005

Computer Lab Opens

First computer laboratory opens, bridging the digital divide and preparing students for a technology-driven future.

2008

First Matric Class

45 students graduate in the inaugural matric class, marking a historic milestone for the school and the community.

2010

New Science Block

State-of-the-art science laboratories added, enabling advanced practical work in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology.

2013

Sports Facilities

New sports grounds and gymnasium constructed, expanding the school's commitment to holistic learner development.

2015

Excellence Award

Named Top School in Nqutu district by the KwaZulu-Natal Department of Basic Education.

2020

Digital Learning

E-learning platforms adopted during COVID-19 pandemic, ensuring uninterrupted education for all learners.

2025

98% Pass Rate

Historic achievement: 98% pass rate in the National Senior Certificate — the school's best result in its 21-year history.
