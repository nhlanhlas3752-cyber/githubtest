Status Guide

What does each status mean?

Understanding where your application is in the review process.

Pending

Your application has been received and is in the queue for review. No action is required from you at this stage.

In Review

Our admissions team is actively reviewing your application and supporting documents. You may be contacted if additional information is needed.

Approved

Congratulations! Your application has been approved. Please visit the school office within 5 working days to complete the formal enrolment process.

Unsuccessful

Unfortunately your application was not successful this cycle. You are welcome to reapply in the next intake. Please contact us for guidance.
