About Us

A Legacy of Excellence in Education

Founded in 2004 by the visionary educator Mr. S. Nguni, Bhekisizwe Senior Secondary School has been the cornerstone of quality education in Nqutu for over two decades. What began as a modest school with a handful of classrooms has grown into one of the region's most respected academic institutions.

As a 100% Black-owned institution, we are deeply proud of our heritage and our unwavering commitment to the communities we serve. Our holistic approach to education nurtures not only academic brilliance but also character, leadership, and a sense of Ubuntu that prepares every learner for the challenges and opportunities of the modern world.
